Walking in the Light

📖 1 John 1:5–10 🎤 Pastor Claire 📅 20 April 2026
✝
"This is the message we have heard from him and declare to you: God is light; in him there is no darkness at all."
1 John 1:5

Introduction

John writes to a community struggling with false teaching — people claiming to follow Jesus but whose lives told a different story. His response isn't complicated doctrine. It's a simple, arresting image: God is light.

What does it mean to walk in the light? And what gets in the way?

1. God's Nature: Pure Light

Light in the ancient world wasn't just illumination — it was purity, truth, holiness. John is saying: there is nothing hidden in God. No shadow, no pretence, no darkness at all.

Key Point: If we say we have fellowship with God yet walk in darkness, we lie. Authenticity before God and with one another is not optional — it's the heartbeat of Christian community.

2. Our Response: Honesty Over Performance

Verse 8 is striking: "If we claim to be without sin, we deceive ourselves." The Christian life isn't about performing righteousness. It's about being honest — before God, with others, and with ourselves.

Walking in the light means bringing our real selves — our failures, fears and failings — into the presence of a God who already knows and already loves.

"But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus, his Son, purifies us from all sin."
1 John 1:7

3. The Gift of Confession

Verse 9 is one of the most liberating verses in the Bible: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness."

Confession isn't punishment — it's the doorway back into the light. It's where shame goes to die and freedom begins.

Application: Is there something you've been keeping in the dark? A sin, a fear, a struggle? Today is a good day to bring it into the light.
