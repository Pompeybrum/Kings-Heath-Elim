Tips for Worship

Making the Most of Worship at Home

🎧
Listen actively
Don't just have it on in the background — find a quiet moment, put headphones in, and really listen to the words.
🙏
Make it personal
Let the lyrics become your own prayer. Worship isn't just singing — it's an expression of your heart to God.
🎵
Sing along
Even at home! The more you sing the melody, the more natural it'll feel on Sunday morning together.
📖
Look up the scripture
Most worship songs are rooted in Bible passages. Finding the source verses deepens the meaning of the words.
