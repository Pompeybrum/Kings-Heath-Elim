Who We Are

Rooted in Kings Heath,
Reaching the World

Kings Heath Elim is a Pentecostal church with deep roots in the local community. We're a place where people encounter God, find friendship and discover their purpose.

We're part of the Elim Pentecostal Church, one of the UK's fastest-growing church movements, with a passion for the Holy Spirit, the Word of God, and reaching our community with the love of Jesus.
