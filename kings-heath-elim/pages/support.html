Why Give?

Generosity is a Kingdom Value

Giving is one of the most powerful ways we can participate in what God is doing. When we give, we're not just paying bills — we're investing in lives being changed, communities being served, and the gospel being shared.

Every gift — however large or small — goes directly towards the work of Kings Heath Elim: our Sunday services, community outreach, youth and children's work, and supporting those in need right here in Birmingham.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

2 Corinthians 9:7
