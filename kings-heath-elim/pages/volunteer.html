Why Serve?

Volunteering is Belonging

At Kings Heath Elim, we believe that every person has been uniquely gifted by God — not just for their own benefit, but for the good of the whole church family and the community around us. When you serve, you're not just filling a rota slot. You're stepping into something that matters. You're building friendships, discovering your gifts, and playing your part in something much bigger than yourself.

This is kingdom work. Whether you're setting out chairs, leading worship, making someone feel welcome at the door, or keeping the building clean — every act of service is an act of love, and God sees it all.
